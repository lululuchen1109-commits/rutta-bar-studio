Questions before you buy

Which Macs does Rutta Bar - R1 work with?

Rutta Bar - R1 works with every Mac equipped with a USB-C or Thunderbolt port — including MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac mini, and Mac Studio — with zero drivers required.

Can I use the neighboring Thunderbolt port and MagSafe 3 at the same time?

Yes. Rutta Bar - R1 extends only 6.00 mm toward the rear. MagSafe 3 remains completely unobstructed, and adjacent Thunderbolt ports accept Apple braided cables and standard 12 mm plugs (~1.08 mm clearance).

Is the mute switch a true hardware mute?

Yes. Sliding the switch back physically breaks the microphone audio path, turns off the top LED, and reveals a bright orange mechanical flag. Its state stays locked even if unplugged.

Does it need charging or a paid subscription?

Neither. R1 draws power straight from your Mac’s USB-C port with no battery to degrade, works as a standard macOS microphone in any app, and includes free core access to the Rutta desktop app.

Do the four finishes match Apple’s Mac aluminum colors?

Silver, Sky Blue, Starlight, and Midnight are anodized and bead-blasted to harmonize with modern Mac aluminum finishes under everyday studio and office lighting.

What is your shipping, return, and warranty policy?

Orders ship within 2 business days with free tracked delivery, backed by a 30-day return window and an 18-month hardware warranty.
